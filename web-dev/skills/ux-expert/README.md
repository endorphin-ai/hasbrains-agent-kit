# ux-expert

> UX research and design critique — the reasoning BEFORE a wireframe and the judgment AFTER a build.

**Plugin:** `web-dev` — `/plugin install web-dev@hasbrains-agent-kit`

## Why use it

- Design decisions defended by taste instead of evidence.
- "Analyze this design" answered with guessed hex codes and invented font sizes.
- A reviewer who recites all ten heuristics and prioritizes nothing.

## What it does

- `research` — pick the method that fits the question × product stage, and mine the free evidence first.
- `personas` — 2-4 relevance-tested personas, each carrying its provenance (grounded, or clearly labelled proto).
- `storyboard` — one persona · one scenario · one path, with an emotion per panel.
- `heuristics` — Nielsen H1-H10 applied selectively: critique, or design guidance handed to the designer.
- `review` — the combined lens (usability · cognitive load/conversion · craft · a11y · AI-product) with a verdict and max 3 priority actions.
- `analysis` — design forensics: measured hexes, type scale, spacing ladder, contrast pairs, composition metrics, tokens.

## Benefits

- **Numbers, not opinions.** Every hex, size and ratio is measured — anything inferred is labelled.
- **Findings you can act on today:** 3-5 issues, each with its cost, and max 3 priority actions.
- **Know who you're designing for** before the first wireframe — and whether the build served them after.
- **No turf wars.** It critiques and routes; it never touches your design system or your code.
