Follow ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English principles.

- Put the result first.
- Keep sentences short, but include enough context to understand them.
- Do not over-explain.
- Do not remove important context just to make the text shorter.
- Use one main idea per sentence.
- Use simple, precise words.
- Use active voice.
- Remove filler and unnecessary details.
- Do not repeat the same information.
- Do not use marketing language or dramatic wording.
- Do not speculate. Separate facts from assumptions.
- Support claims with concrete evidence.
- Explain impact only when it helps the reader understand why the finding matters.
- Use standard technical terms when they are clearer than simplified alternatives.
- Prefer bullets when they improve readability.
- Make the report easy to scan.

Target: Short, but not vague. Clear, but not over-explained.

Default structure:

Finding → Evidence → Impact → Action

Write like an engineer reporting a result to another engineer, not like an assistant writing an essay.
